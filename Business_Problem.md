# Business Problem

## Retail Decision Intelligence

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data containing information about products, customers, quantities, prices, dates, and countries. However, raw transaction data alone does not provide an immediate view of business performance or customer behavior.

The objective of this project is to transform retail transaction data into actionable business insights that can support sales monitoring, product prioritization, customer analysis, and revenue planning.

## Key Business Questions

### Sales Performance

- What is the overall revenue generated?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which countries contribute the most revenue?
- How does sales performance vary across different periods?

### Product Performance

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products have the highest sales volume?
- Which products contribute most significantly to total revenue?
- Which products should receive greater business attention based on ABC classification?

### Customer Behaviour

- Which customers are the most valuable?
- How recently do customers purchase?
- How frequently do customers purchase?
- How much do customers spend?
- How can customers be segmented according to their purchasing behavior?

### Market Basket Analysis

- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- What product associations can be identified from transaction history?
- Can these associations support cross-selling opportunities?

### Revenue Forecasting

- What are the historical revenue trends?
- Is there evidence of recurring seasonal patterns?
- What does the forecast suggest about future revenue?

## Analytical Objective

The project aims to answer these questions through an end-to-end analytics workflow combining:

- Python for data preparation and exploratory analysis
- MySQL for structured business analysis
- RFM analysis for customer segmentation
- Market Basket Analysis for product associations
- Pareto and ABC analysis for product prioritization
- Time-series forecasting for revenue planning
- Power BI for interactive business reporting

## Expected Business Value

The resulting analysis can help stakeholders:

- Monitor sales performance
- Identify high-performing products
- Prioritize important products and inventory
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify potential cross-selling opportunities
- Evaluate historical revenue trends
- Support future sales planning using revenue forecasts

The final Power BI dashboard brings these analytical outputs together into an interactive decision-support interface.
